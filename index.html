Do you guarantee Google will remove a fake review?

No. Nobody can — Google alone decides. We maximize the quality of your report and appeal with policy citations and visit/POS no-match evidence.

How is GBP Shield different from GatherUp Review Defense?

GatherUp is a broader review platform, and Review Defense runs roughly $50–$375/mo. Stay there if it already covers you. GBP Shield is narrower: compliant post-visit velocity, POS/CRM-backed evidence packs, and restriction/attack recovery for high-ticket dental, med spa, and HVAC businesses.

Is this compliant with Google’s review policies?

Yes, by design. We don’t incentivize, gate, or buy reviews, and we don’t auto-post AI replies. Asks go out after real visits, are optional for the customer, and are velocity-throttled.

What if someone is extorting me with fake 1-star reviews?

Don’t pay. Document every message, report it through Google’s merchant extortion channels, and back the report with an evidence pack and a clear report trail. Concierge includes an attack war-room playbook.

Do you sell NFC cards, review kiosks, or AI reply bots?

No — they’re out of scope on purpose. We don’t sell NFC cards, policy-breaking QR gimmicks, or automated AI replies.

What do the first 20 waitlist members get?

The first 20 qualifying dental, med spa, and HVAC owners get a free evidence-pack review (if you have an open fake-review case) and a 15-minute GBP risk teardown. Validation pricing is locked for 6 months if you convert.

Does joining the waitlist cost anything?

No. Joining is free and doesn’t commit you to a plan. We’ll only email you about GBP Shield early access and your evidence pack.
