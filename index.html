Do you guarantee Google will remove a fake review?

No. Nobody can — Google alone decides. We make your report and appeal as strong as they can be, with policy citations and visit/POS no-match evidence.

How is GBP Shield different from GatherUp Review Defense?

GatherUp is a broader review platform, and Review Defense runs roughly $50–$375/mo. If it already covers you, stay there. GBP Shield is narrower: evidence packs built from your own visit and POS records, for high-ticket dental, med spa, and HVAC businesses.
